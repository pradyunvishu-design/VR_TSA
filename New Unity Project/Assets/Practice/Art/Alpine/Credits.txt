North Ridge winter refuge
Practice environment for FIRST180

SnowlineForest.obj incorporates four mesh designs from Nature Kit 2.1 by Kenney.
tree_pineTallA_detailed, tree_pineRoundC, tree_pineSmallA, stone_largeA
https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
Distribution by Kenney: https://opengameart.org/content/nature-kit
License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The original license accompanies this file.

The four meshes were resized, arranged into groves and scree, remapped to the North Ridge palette, and given face-fitted asymmetric snow cover. The refuge, furniture, lamps, wayfinding, terrain, trail, mountain skyline, snow particles and palette were created for this practice scene. Source~/build_shelter.py rebuilds the editable mesh groups from the original ZIP with Python, numpy and Pillow.
