For the practice build, the workshop props were exported in static poses, reduced for real-time rendering and paired with 512px, softly desaturated albedos. Floor scans were downsampled and contrast-adjusted. The port layout, road taper, fabrication cell, safety guards, bay architecture, two-colour wall treatments and signage are project-specific arrangements.
