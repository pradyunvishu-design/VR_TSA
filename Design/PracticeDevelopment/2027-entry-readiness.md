## The national entry package

**Recording.** Time the actual export from its first visible image or audible sound through its last. The permitted range is 2:00–3:00. A practical editing target is 2:30. Show actual VR use, immediate training feedback, an independent certification decision and the resulting analytics. Include sounds from the simulation. Voiceover alone is insufficient. Keep all footage computer-generated. The event excludes live action, whiteboard video, PowerPoint and purchased-element animation apps. Test the final video URL in a signed-out browser on another device. Let it play through once, with sound and no further input. A link requiring access approval or login fails the access check. [Event guide and recording rules](https://www.pca-tsa.org/HS/HS%20-%20VR.pdf#page=2)

**Portfolio.** Assemble one PDF in this order. Obtain the two official forms from the 2027–2028 high-school Forms Appendix in the chapter’s membership materials. [Event portfolio requirements](https://www.pca-tsa.org/HS/HS%20-%20VR.pdf#page=2)

1. Title page with event, conference city/state and year, one page. For nationals, the 2027 location is Orlando, Florida.
2. Contents with final page numbers.
3. Purpose and intended audience, one page.
4. Students’ hand-drawn storyboard, scanned legibly. Annotate scene timings, transitions, effects, sound and dialogue against the actual cut.
5. References for all material used, including free and original material as applicable.
6. Written permissions for copyrighted material where applicable.
7. Software and hardware actually used, one page.
8. Completed Student Copyright Checklist, one page.
9. Completed Resources and AI Reflection, up to four pages.

Use the team or student ID where identification is required. Inspect filenames, PDF author fields, visible screenshots, credits, video title and hosting account for accidental student or school identifiers. Retain accurate source attribution. Ask the advisor how to submit any necessary signed letters that reveal identities. Gulf Coast and Texas also require a direct online video link, a common video format playable in VLC on Windows, and a PDF up to 30 MB. They disallow post-deadline changes. Test the exported file in VLC, then test its hosted version. The national platform also specifies a 30 MB PDF limit. [Regional upload rules](https://datastudio.google.com/reporting/c24b34cd-fb2f-45fa-a594-d1401ab1950f/page/REskF) · [State upload rules](https://datastudio.google.com/reporting/a8e94bde-130e-4630-b905-82319d07a645/page/REskF) Save the upload confirmation and retest the submitted links. [Texas general rules](https://texastsa.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/General-Rules-and-Regulations.pdf#page=4) · [National upload instructions](https://tsaweb.org/competitions/competitive-event-preconference-submission-requirements)

**Consequences differ.** The rating form gates evaluation on playable material and a portfolio. The event has specific disqualification language for originality, citation and permission violations. Runtime outside the permitted window carries a five-point deduction per 15-second interval. An incomplete portfolio also affects scoring. [Event rules and rating form](https://www.pca-tsa.org/HS/HS%20-%20VR.pdf#page=4)
