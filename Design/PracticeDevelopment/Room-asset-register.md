**Interior revision.** Switchback Coffee reuses the existing Kenney round tables, chairs, mugs and plants for two window-side settings. Its counter motif uses three Unity blocks with the existing limestone finish. Relay Hall rotates one communal table, both benches and their place settings together. The opposite table sits farther from the serving hatches. The new venue names, signs and numbered serving hatches were AI-assisted edits to the practice project. This revision introduced no outside models, textures, logos or audio.

## Competition status

The linked **2027–2028 national VR guide** requires the participants’ original work, credit for outside sources, and written permission for copyrighted materials where applicable. Purchased content is prohibited, including purchased models, textures and music. The guide permits GenAI subject to the General Rules and requires a Resources and AI Reflection. Document the actual AI-assisted contributions, including this practice build’s scene assembly and layout changes. [Event requirements, pp. 382–383](https://www.pca-tsa.org/HS/HS%20-%20VR.pdf#page=2)

The public licenses establish reuse rights for the identified scenery. Final entry eligibility, how the team obtained its assets, and the student authorship of the entry still require confirmation. Have the chapter advisor check the intended event year, any state-specific rules, and whether the retained license notices satisfy the event’s permission documentation.
